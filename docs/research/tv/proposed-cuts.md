# Proposed Cuts: TV, One Critical Pass

*A reread of all 784 shows asking one question: does this earn its place on an anti-library that's supposed to emphasize quality and the frontier? Nothing is cut yet. Say "cut them all," name the tiers, or pick.*

**Verdict first.** The shelves are right; none should go. The net is too wide in four specific places, and they're all padding of the same kind: shows that are *there* rather than *recommended*. Cutting everything below takes the list from 784 to about 690, still bigger than the film list, and every remaining row would have a reason to exist.

**Tiers:** ✂✂✂ cut, no argument · ✂✂ cut unless you object · ✂ cut or mark seen, your call

---

## 1. Rows with no hook (62 shows)

A row on this list is a title, a year, a creator and *one line on why*. Sixty-two rows have no line. I wrote them in because they were on the channel or in the canon, not because I had a reason you'd watch them. Most are from two shelves: the Nick at Nite classics and the documentary wing. Rule: a show without a hook is a show I couldn't make the case for. Cut them, except the eight below that deserve a hook written instead.

**Keep, and write the missing hook (8):**
- **Dragnet** (1951–1959). Jack Webb's flat delivery is the ancestor of deadpan police comedy, Police Squad! included, and Square One's Mathnet parodies it.
- **Alfred Hitchcock Presents** (1955–1962). Hitchcock's dry intros are a one-man anti-comedy act; the anthology format feeds Twilight Zone and Inside No. 9.
- **The Fugitive** (1963–1967). The first series finale that the whole country watched; the "endings" thread.
- **Sanford and Son** (1972–1977). Steptoe and Son is on the list; this is its American half, and Redd Foxx.
- **The Carol Burnett Show** (1967–1978). The last great variety show; the Harvey Korman/Tim Conway breaks are the live-TV version of Curb's improvisation.
- **Roots** (1977). Still the most-watched miniseries in US history; the format's origin.
- **The Civil War** (1990). Ken Burns' method, defined. Keep one Burns here and let Hemingway/Twain carry the rest.
- **Jazz** (2001). For the music list.

**Cut (54):**
- Classic TV: The Beverly Hillbillies, The Addams Family, The Munsters, Bewitched, I Dream of Jeannie, Mister Ed, Gilligan's Island, F Troop, Father Knows Best, The Adventures of Ozzie and Harriet, The Odd Couple, Maude, The Jeffersons, Rhoda, Rowan & Martin's Laugh-In, The Tonight Show Starring Johnny Carson, Naked City, Perry Mason, The Untouchables, The Avengers. *You've seen the ones that matter on Nick at Nite already; the rest are channel filler. Green Acres, Get Smart, Dobie Gillis, Honeymooners and Bilko carry the shelf.*
- British: Dad's Army, Porridge, Only Fools and Horses, Phoenix Nights, Catastrophe. *Beloved in Britain; none is a frontier for you.*
- World: Fauda. *An action show; Prisoners of War and Our Boys cover Israel better.*
- Limited series: Brideshead Revisited, The Pacific, Mildred Pierce, Top of the Lake, A Very English Scandal, Sharp Objects, Unbelievable, When They See Us, The Underground Railroad, Mare of Easttown. *Good television, every one; none connects to anything else on your shelves.*
- Documentary: Civilisation, The Brain with David Eagleman, Life on Earth, Planet Earth, The Vietnam War, The Power of Nightmares, Making a Murderer, The Keepers, Hip-Hop Evolution, Soul Train, The Midnight Special, 1971, Song Exploder. *Private Life of Plants carries Attenborough; Century of the Self carries Curtis; Paradise Lost and The Jinx carry true crime.*
- Kids: Salute Your Shorts, Doug, Rugrats, Legends of the Hidden Temple, Captain Kangaroo. *Nostalgia without an argument.*

## What I would not cut

- The ★ picks (168). Every one has an argument.
- The Core and TGIF shelves; seen is seen.
- Anime (17) and Korea (20); both are frontier for you and both are tied to the physics, consciousness and endings threads.
- The Vonnegut, Fielder, Chris Elliott and Larry David lineages; they're the spine.
- The stand-up shelf; it's small and it came off your bookshelf.

**Totals:** 54 + 9 + 16 + 8 + 10 + 27 + 5 = about 129 proposed cuts, of which about 95 are ✂✂✂ or ✂✂. Cutting all of them leaves about 655 shows; cutting only the first two tiers leaves about 690.
