# Music Anti-Library: Proposed Cuts

*Applied 2026-10-11: groups D and E (28 albums) were cut. Groups A, B, C and F were kept.*

*Then the canon cut, also 2026-10-11: "The canon by decade" went from 875 albums to 352 using the seven tests. The 523 cut albums are in music-archive.md, each with its reason. R.E.M. *Murmur* and *Automatic for the People* are ticked as heard. The list is now 1,337 entries.*

*A final critical pass, October 2026. 166 proposals against 1,888 entries. Nothing has been removed yet.*

## The verdict

The net is wide in one place: **the canon by decade**, which is 877 of the 1,888 entries. That section inherited the breadth of *1001 Albums*, and much of it is either music you've absorbed from radio and film without choosing it, or second-tier albums by artists who aren't your people. The rest of the list holds up: the frontiers (jazz, classical, world) are where you said you want to go, the bridge and artist paths are tuned to your answers, and the threads section is the most "you" part of the whole thing.

So the cut is targeted, not across the board. Taking all of it would delete **133** albums, bringing the list to about **1,755**, with the canon dropping from 877 to about 790. Another **33** albums would stay on the list but be ticked as heard, since you have almost certainly absorbed them already. That is 166 fewer unheard albums in all.

**What I'm not proposing to cut, and why**

- **Jazz and classical** (360 entries). You named classical your biggest frontier and jazz your deepest love. If anything these are thin.
- **Deep runs on your own favorites** (17 Waits, 13 Miles, 11 Coltrane, 7 Mingus, 7 Radiohead). Depth on artists you love is the point of an anti-library, not bloat.
- **Hip-hop's literary wing,** except the weakest four. You said you love hip-hop, and the canon was thin on exactly that side.
- **Starred picks and anything ticked heard.** None of the proposals below carry a ★.

Each group below says why. Strike any line you want to keep, or reply with a group letter ("keep C", "cut everything but E").
