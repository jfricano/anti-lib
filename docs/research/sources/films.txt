## The Slaughterhouse-Five family tree
The World According to Garp | 1982 | George Roy Hill | Same director as Slaughterhouse-Five, and John Irving was Vonnegut's student at Iowa. Irving is already on the book list; this closes the loop.
Slap Shot | 1977 | George Roy Hill | Profane, funny, violent hockey. The Hill film closest to my wheelhouse.
Back to School | 1986 | Alan Metter | Rodney Dangerfield hires Kurt Vonnegut (playing himself) to write his Vonnegut paper. Gets a D.
A Ghost Story | 2017 | David Lowery | A sheeted ghost watches centuries pass through one house. The most unstuck-in-time film since Slaughterhouse-Five.
Peppermint Candy | 2000 | Lee Chang-dong | A man's life told backward through twenty years of Korean history. Lee is already on the list twice; this is his Billy Pilgrim film.
The Razor's Edge | 1984 | John Byrum | Bill Murray's passion project: a WWI vet goes looking for meaning in India. He made Ghostbusters to get this financed.
Stranger Than Fiction | 2006 | Marc Forster | Will Ferrell hears a novelist narrating his life and learns he's scheduled to die. Metafiction with a heart. The Other Guys' star in a Vonnegut plot.
Dark Star | 1974 | John Carpenter | Astronauts argue phenomenology with a bomb that wants to detonate. Absurdist space comedy, made for nothing.
Miracle Mile | 1988 | Steve De Jarnatt | A man answers a payphone in LA and learns the missiles land in seventy minutes. Cat's Cradle as a date movie.
The Atomic Cafe | 1982 | Loader, Rafferty & Rafferty | A collage of 1950s civil-defense films. Gallows humor with no narrator needed.
When the Wind Blows | 1986 | Jimmy Murakami | An animated elderly couple follows government pamphlets through nuclear winter. Raymond Briggs.
Palm Springs | 2020 | Max Barbakow | A time loop at a wedding. The funniest recent descendant of Groundhog Day.
Timecrimes | 2007 | Nacho Vigalondo | A small, mean, perfect time-loop thriller.
Death Becomes Her | 1992 | Robert Zemeckis | Immortality as a curse, played for slapstick. Vonnegut's "Fortitude" as a Meryl Streep comedy.
## Hal Ashby, complete

Shampoo | 1975 | Hal Ashby | Warren Beatty's hairdresser on election night 1968.
Coming Home | 1978 | Hal Ashby | Vietnam comes back.
Bound for Glory | 1976 | Hal Ashby | Woody Guthrie. For the music list.
